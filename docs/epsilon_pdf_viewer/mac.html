Epsilon for Mac

Version 1.4.6, November 2026

Epsilon PDF is the Epsilon PDF viewer as a Mac app: the same formula search, copy as LaTeX or Mathematica, outline and peek, for the PDFs on your Mac. It reloads a PDF in place when LaTeX writes it again, and goes from the PDF to your source and back with SyncTeX.

  • macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.
  • Local PDFs open from Finder, each in its own window.
  • Free, and it keeps itself up to date.

Download for Mac

Install

  1. Unzip the download and drag Epsilon PDF to your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. The app is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks its first launch once.

macOS 15 Sequoia and later

  1. Double-click the app. macOS says “Epsilon PDF” Not Opened; click Done.
  2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security. Under Security, next to “Epsilon PDF” was blocked to protect your Mac, click Open Anyway.
  3. Enter your password or use Touch ID, then click Open Anyway again.

macOS 13 Ventura and 14 Sonoma

  1. Control-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.

After that it opens like any other app.

Make it your PDF app

The first time it opens, Epsilon PDF asks whether to become your PDF app; Settings › General has the same Make Default button. Or by hand:

  1. In Finder, select any PDF and choose File › Get Info (⌘ I).
  2. Under Open with, choose Epsilon PDF, then click Change All… and Continue.

To go back, do the same and choose Preview.

Writing LaTeX

When the PDF changes on disk, after a LaTeX run, it is replaced in place: the page, zoom, sidebar and outline stay where they were. File › Reload from Disk (⌘ R) does it by hand.

With a PDF built with -synctex=1, ⌥-click, ⇧ ⌘-click or right-click › Show in Editor opens that line of the source in your editor, even when the PDF's window is behind it. The editor is the app that opens .tex files, or the one chosen in Settings › LaTeX: Sublime Text, VS Code, Cursor, VSCodium, Zed, TextMate, BBEdit or a command of your own. From the editor back to the PDF, the editor runs displayline, with Skim's arguments: the PDF scrolls to the line and marks it in blue.

Inside beamer frames and align, gather, multline or split, SyncTeX files the first lines under the last, so a line near the start can land just before it. Skim does the same.

Set up your editor

Your editor finds the line in the PDF through displayline, inside the app; -g keeps the PDF behind the editor. Build with latexmk -pdf -synctex=1, or your editor's own build, which usually has SyncTeX on.

VS Code (LaTeX Workshop), in settings.json:

"latex-workshop.view.pdf.viewer": "external",
"latex-workshop.view.pdf.external.synctex.command":
  "/Applications/Epsilon PDF.app/Contents/Resources/displayline",
"latex-workshop.view.pdf.external.synctex.args": ["-g", "%LINE%", "%PDF%", "%TEX%"]

Sublime Text (LaTeXTools), in LaTeXTools.sublime-settings:

"viewer": "command",
"viewer_settings": {"osx": {
  "forward_sync_command": ["/Applications/Epsilon PDF.app/Contents/Resources/displayline",
                           "-g", "$line", "$pdf_file", "$src_file"],
  "view_command": ["open", "-a", "Epsilon PDF", "$pdf_file"]
}}

Vim (vimtex):

let g:vimtex_view_method = 'general'
let g:vimtex_view_general_viewer = '/Applications/Epsilon PDF.app/Contents/Resources/displayline'
let g:vimtex_view_general_options = '-g @line @pdf @tex'

Emacs (AUCTeX): an entry in TeX-view-program-list that runs displayline -g %n %o %b.

On the Mac

  • Open Recent, and PDFs dragged onto the Dock icon or a window.
  • The Mac's print panel, and Save a Copy.
  • Settings in their own window (⌘ ,).
  • Trackpad pinch that follows your fingers.
  • Back and forward inside the PDF always on (⌘ [ and ⌘ ]).
  • Your place in each file kept; leaving presentation mode returns to the page you ended on.
  • Copy, Copy as LaTeX and Copy as Mathematica in the Edit menu and the right-click menu.

Not yet

  • Page editing: rotate, delete, reorder, combine, crop.
  • Saving in place: highlights and notes are kept only with Save a Copy, and are lost when the window closes or the file changes.
  • Preview's own markup tools; the viewer's highlight, text, drawing and signature tools are there.
  • Tabs, Share and AppleScript.

PDFs downloaded from the web carry macOS's quarantine mark, and Finder won't open them in an app that isn't notarized. Open them with File › Open, or drag them onto an open window.

Updates and removal

Once a day the app checks this website for a new version, downloads it in the background and installs it when you quit; Epsilon PDF › Check for Updates… checks at once.

To remove it:

  1. If it is your PDF app, choose another first (above, choosing Preview).
  2. Quit Epsilon PDF (⌘ Q).
  3. Drag Epsilon PDF from your Applications folder to the Trash.
  4. To remove what it keeps too (its settings and your place in each file, saved signatures, the updater's cache), run in Terminal: 
    defaults delete nl.uva.epsilon.pdfapp
rm -rf ~/Library/WebKit/nl.uva.epsilon.pdfapp ~/Library/Caches/nl.uva.epsilon.pdfapp ~/Library/HTTPStorages/nl.uva.epsilon.pdfapp

Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac: settings, your place in each file and saved signatures. The app reads your PDFs from disk and sends them nowhere. Its only network request is the daily update check to this website, which sends nothing about you or your Mac. Links in a PDF open in your browser. See also the privacy policy.
